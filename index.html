Who We Are

Neighbors Helping Neighbors in an Economy of Care

Lummi Island Mutual Aid is an informal, grassroots network dedicated to matching direct requests for aid with local donors. We are an open channel of care—sharing groceries, prepared meals, emergency support, and essential supplies.

LIMA is not a 501(c)3 non-profit. Donations are not tax-deductible; instead, they represent genuine, direct gift-giving and solidarity rooted in our unique island geography.

Strictly Confidential

Names and contact details are never shared publicly or online.

Flexible Support

From gift cards to grocery drops, meals, and practical volunteer hands.

Process

How The Network Works

Simple, respectful, and private connection between islanders.

1

Share or Request

Fill out an online request or visit The Islander / LIMA Community Pantry to pick up a physical paper form. Drop completed paper slips into the secure drop box at The Islander.

2

Confidential Matching

LIMA organizers match requests with grocery food cards, community donations, or local volunteer cooks. Your name and phone number stay strictly private and are never shared publicly.

3

Discreet Distribution

Pick up designated packages at the Pantry, redeem digital grocery food cards, or coordinate directly with providers for meal handoffs.

Ways to Give

Support Fellow Islanders

Every contribution keeps our neighbors nourished. Donate grocery food cards, supply the pantry, or participate in the Adopt-a-Family program.

Grocery Food Cards

High-impact, immediate assistance for fresh groceries

LIMA matches donated grocery food cards (gift cards) directly with island families in need.

The Islander Store (On-Island)

Donate in person at the register (cash, card, check) and state the funds are for LIMA food card recipients. We coordinate with Islander staff to assign funds to families' store accounts.

Full Bloom Farm (On-Island)

Purchase gift cards online with lummiislandmutualaid@gmail.com as recipient. We transfer them for redemption at the farm kiosk.

Mainland Stores (Digital or Physical)

Fred Meyer, Haggen, WinCo, Walmart, Community Food Co-op, Trader Joe's, or Costco. Send digital e-cards to our email, or email us to arrange collection of physical gift cards.
