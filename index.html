The LIMA Community Pantry

  • Location: 2141 Granger Way, Lummi Island, WA 98262
  • Parking: Available on-site in designated areas
  • Available: Shelf-stable groceries, hygiene & personal care items (no dairy or refrigeration).
  • Meal Pickups: Labeled bags for pre-arranged meal or special requests.

Paper copies of request and donation slips are available inside the pantry and at The Islander Store. Completed slips can be dropped in the collection box at The Islander.

Ways to Give & Share

LIMA operates as an informal mutual aid collective and gift economy. We are not a 501(c)3 non-profit; donations are voluntary gifts of community care.

🏪

On-Island Accounts

The Islander Store: Donate cash, check, or card at checkout. Mention it is for LIMA food card recipients to credit store accounts.

Full Bloom Farm: Order gift certificates online with lummiislandmutualaid@gmail.com as recipient; we route vouchers to families for farm stand kiosk use.

💳

Grocery Cards

Donate physical or digital grocery cards from Fred Meyer, Haggen, WinCo, Walmart, Community Food Co-op, Trader Joe's, or Costco.

Send digital cards to lummiislandmutualaid@gmail.com or email us to arrange physical pickup.

🍲

Meals & Services

Share your skills, offer a home-cooked meal for an island neighbor in need, or lend a hand with pantry distribution.

We match meal providers directly with families to coordinate pickup or delivery privately.

Standard Distribution & "Adopt a Family" Guidelines

Household Size Standard Food Card Distribution Monthly "Adopt a Family" Sponsorship
Individuals $50 $200 / month
Family of 2 $100 $400 / month
Family of 3 $200 $600 / month
Family of 4 or more $200+ $800 / month

Note: Recipients can submit up to 4 requests per month as needs arise.
