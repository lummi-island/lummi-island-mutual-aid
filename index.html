How It Works

A direct neighbor-to-neighbor gift economy matching goods, meals, and assistance.

1. Request or Offer

You can offer or request services, groceries, food cards, or meals. Email us directly or drop off a paper form.

Paper donation and request forms are available at The Islander Store and inside our Community Pantry.

2. Complete Privacy

We will never share your name or contact info online.

We only collect these details privately behind the scenes so we can coordinate direct matches for goods and services.

3. Pick Up & Connect

If you request a prepared meal, we will put you in touch with the meal provider directly to arrange pickup or delivery.

Dedicated pantry requests are bagged and labeled privately at the Granger Way pantry.

Donations & Grocery Food Cards

LIMA is not a 501(c)3 non-profit; contributions are not tax-deductible. They are gifts shared in the spirit of community care.

Grocery Food Cards

We match donor-funded food cards with island requests. Cards can be purchased from:

  • The Islander Store: Donate via cash, check, or card at the checkout counter. Staff will credit the funds to dedicated LIMA recipient store accounts.
  • Full Bloom Farm: Purchase gift certificates online with lummiislandmutualaid@gmail.com as recipient; we will route them directly to recipients to use at the kiosk stand.
  • Off-Island Stores: Fred Meyer, Haggen, WinCo, Walmart, Community Food Co-op, Trader Joe's, or Costco.
  • Digital Cards: Email e-cards to lummiislandmutualaid@gmail.com and we will confirm receipt and print them for distribution.
